A 15.9-second vertical Short cut by an AI agent from Rockstar Games' official Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1: Lucia and Jason's first robbery — the store door bursts open and both guns come up in the trailer's climax shot. Entered in make.fun's "Clip GTA 6 for the most YouTube views" bounty ($500, 20 paid places, ranked by YouTube views).

How it was made, in one recorded session: six candidate moments from Trailer 1 were scored on an 8-criterion clip rubric (hook, payoff, loopability, reaction, legibility, audio, length, packaging); the robbery won at 66/80. Three edits of it were then built — a loop cut, a real-time cut, and a beat-ramp cut — rendered with ffmpeg at 1080x1920, and audited on their actual frames. The loop cut won the audit at 66/80 (SHIP): the payoff is in frame one, the make.fun pitch ("Make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun" + the bounty URL) rides the slow-motion pass over moving footage, and the final shot replays the opening so the Short loops.

`VIDEO.json` holds a placeholder URL until the clip is published on YouTube; the published Short's address replaces it before submission.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
